NITTE BUS TRACKER — CORRECTION NOTES

1. Bus.java now validates timetable times and handles null/blank text safely.
2. Afternoon timetable entries 1:00–5:59 are converted to 13:00–17:59 internally.
3. Search time filters are validated as 24-hour values.
4. A filter range crossing midnight is handled correctly instead of swapping its endpoints.
5. Original timetable entries and labels have been preserved; they have not been independently verified.

Run:
  mvn spring-boot:run

Then open:
  http://localhost:8080

Requirements: Java 17+ and Maven 3.8+.
